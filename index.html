About Basic Marine Engineering

Formulated out of a massive real-world demand for accessible, high-quality, and animated shipboard educational systems. Built specifically for marine mechanical engineering cadets, fourth engineers, electrical officers, and sea-faring experts.

Our ultimate mission is simple: demystify the complex hydraulic loops, thermodynamic cycles, power transmission gearsets, and strict marine regulatory procedures, translating complex ship blueprints into simplified and interactive walkthroughs.
